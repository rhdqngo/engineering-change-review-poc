# Non-Game UI Context

For non-game products, prioritize the core task, frequency of use, cost of error, trust, and user expertise.

## Product-purpose modifiers

- `task-completion`
- `content-consumption`
- `conversion`
- `collaboration`
- `administration`
- `monitoring`
- `account-management`

## Required questions

- What recurring job is most important to the user?
- Is the action frequent or rare?
- Is the audience novice, mixed, or expert?
- Is the action reversible?
- Does it involve money, identity, permissions, deletion, or legal submission?
- Does the user need evidence or explanation before choosing?
- Does the workflow require comparison, bulk action, or collaboration state?

## High-stakes actions

- Show target, scope, amount, and result close to the action.
- Show the actual change and reversibility instead of repeating generic warning copy.
- Preserve input and context after failure.
- After completion, provide a record, receipt, undo path, or next step when appropriate.
- Do not let promotional emphasis overpower operational or security actions.

## Expert density

- Frequent workflows may support shortcuts, bulk operations, and high-density presentation.
- Density does not mean removing hierarchy.
- Keep novice paths discoverable without making expert workflows unnecessarily slow.
- Choose tables, lists, or canvases according to data relationships.

## Trust and explanation

For recommendations, automated decisions, predictions, prices, inventory, permissions, and system state, provide the rationale, scope, and freshness needed to make a decision. An “AI recommendation” label alone is not a basis for trust.

## Forms and errors

- Place errors close to their cause.
- Preserve input after recoverable failure.
- Mark required and optional fields consistently.
- When needed, explain why a control is disabled instead of presenting only a disabled control.
- Distinguish autosave, draft save, and completed submission.

## Accessibility and localization

- Support keyboard-only workflows and meaningful focus order.
- Do not communicate errors, success, warnings, or selection with color alone.
- Account for zoom, long translations, and date, number, and currency formats.
- Do not solve touch and mouse density differences by scaling the same layout mechanically.
