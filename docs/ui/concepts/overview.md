# Demo UI Concept Directions

All directions used the same synthetic DIR-01 probe content before production UI work. Probe outputs are explicitly not evaluation evidence.

## A — Fixed-Candidate Review Docket (selected)

- Dominant object: immutable six-row candidate docket.
- Interaction: select a row; its evidence and three verification gates remain visible in a 62/38 evidence desk.
- Strength: makes candidate identity, selection added value, and fail-closed behavior simultaneously comparable.

## B — Evidence-Gated Candidate Ledger

- Dominant object: vertical ledger with one inline proof fold open at a time.
- Interaction: expand a row in place.
- Trade-off: excellent reading continuity, but row expansion makes cross-candidate comparison less stable and moves proof geometry.

## C — Proof-Gated Case Hearing

- Dominant object: procedural four-stage hearing and decision receipt.
- Interaction: advance through Case & Freeze, Baseline, Verified Review, and Receipt.
- Trade-off: strongest audit narrative, but hides the fixed six-candidate comparison behind steps and adds friction for repeated expert review.

## Selection record

Direction A is provisional. It most directly exposes the experiment's controlled variable: the candidate docket never changes, while the proposed arm adds only review disposition and verified evidence. Direction B lost on comparison stability; Direction C lost on expert throughput. No direction is approved precedent yet.

### Divergence evidence

- A vs B: persistent side evidence desk versus inline single-open fold; fixed split versus moving vertical expansion.
- A vs C: browse-and-compare docket versus staged procedural receipt; all candidates visible versus contextual manifest.
- B vs C: evidence unfolds inside source rows versus an explicit step transition and receipt.
